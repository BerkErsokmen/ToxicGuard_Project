# 2. REALISTIC CONSTRAINTS AND CONDITIONS

## 2.1. Sustainable Development Goal

The ToxicGuard project is aligned with **UN Sustainable Development Goal 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions**. SDG 16 targets the promotion of peaceful and inclusive societies, access to justice for all, and the building of effective and accountable institutions. In the context of digital infrastructure, online platforms serve as critical public institutions of communication. When these spaces are overrun by hate speech, harassment, and coordinated abuse, the institutional integrity of digital civic life is directly undermined.

By engineering an automated tool that helps moderators identify and mitigate toxic content, ToxicGuard contributes to reducing communicative violence in digital institutions, supporting fairer and safer spaces for public interaction. Additionally, the transparent, explainable design of the system — built around human-in-the-loop oversight rather than opaque autonomous enforcement — aligns with SDG 16's emphasis on accountable governance and accessible justice. The system also supports SDG 16.10, which calls for ensuring public access to information and the protection of fundamental freedoms, by minimizing false positives that could unjustly suppress legitimate speech.

*Reference: United Nations SDG 16 — https://turkiye.un.org/tr/sdgs*
